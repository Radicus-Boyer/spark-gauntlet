# Spark Gauntlet

A retro bullet-hell boss rush. Fight 10 bosses in a row, spend shards on stats between fights, pick relics, and unlock new classes.

## Play

**https://radicus-boyer.github.io/spark-gauntlet/**

The link picks the right version for your device automatically.

- `phone.html` is the phone version: drag to move, on-screen FIRE / GUARD / SPELL / SPECIAL buttons.
- `desktop.html` is the PC version: keyboard controls and fullscreen.

## PC controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Arrows / WASD | Move |
| Z | Shoot / Guard |
| X | Spell |
| C | Special |
| Esc | Pause |
| M | Sound on/off |
| F | Fullscreen |

## Saves

Your progress is saved in your own browser on your own device. Phone and PC don't share saves, and clearing your browser data erases them.
